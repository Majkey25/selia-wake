The app's Settings → Privacy & support contains an offline English/Czech disclosure. The public policy covers data types and purpose, device-to-device transfers, camera and network permissions, secure storage, retention, removal, contact, separate Tailscale service, website hosting and support correspondence.

There is no Selia Wake account creation, advertising, billing or analytics SDK. Account deletion for a Selia Wake account is therefore not applicable; the data-removal page explains local controls and keeps Tailscale account management separate. Do not claim that forgetting one pairing revokes other copies, that deleting a portable executable clears OS secure storage, or that the local LAN broadcast is encrypted.

The Data safety questionnaire must reflect the actual release and Google's collection/sharing definitions, including user-directed peer transfers and any applicable exceptions. Do not copy SeliaScan's advertising or billing declarations. Other Play Console declarations, store graphics, testing eligibility and production review are separate from publishing these pages. Keep distribution in internal testing until a production release is expressly requested.
