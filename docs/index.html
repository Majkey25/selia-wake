Two roles. Your devices.

The Waker

An Android phone, laptop, desktop, or Raspberry Pi that stays awake on the target computer's local network. It receives a signed request and sends the local wake packet.

User

The device you carry. Import the Waker's private pairing link or scan its QR code on Android. Save, then press Wake. The CLI can send the same request.

The sleeping computer cannot be its own Waker. It does not need Selia Wake installed, but its firmware and connected network adapter must support wake.

Before you press Wake

What does the pairing link share?

The Waker's address, name and secret. A QR code contains the same information. Review the name and address, then save. Pairing does not connect Tailscale for you. Share codes only with trusted devices.

Does “Packet sent” mean the computer started?

It confirms the Waker's UDP send operation. The app cannot confirm that the target received the packet or booted. Check its state separately.

Can I use an old laptop as The Waker?

Yes. Keep it powered, awake and connected to the target LAN. Keep the GUI or CLI relay running. Check the laptop's lid-close and sleep settings. No automatic startup is installed.

Do I open a router port?

No. Use Tailscale between your devices. The relay uses TCP 45879 and local UDP 9. Restrict the listener to intended peers with firewall and tailnet rules. Never publish it through router forwarding or Tailscale Funnel.

Where is my data stored?

The GUI uses OS secure storage. The CLI and firmware have their own local storage, described in the privacy policy. Selia Wake has no developer-operated relay or analytics service.
