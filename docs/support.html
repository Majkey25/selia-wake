Support

Matěj Teplý, publishing as Majkey / MajkeyLab, maintains Selia Wake. For app help and privacy requests, email majkeylab@gmail.com. English and Czech support are available.

Before reporting a problem

  • Read the setup guide, including wireless wake.
  • Keep The Waker awake on the target LAN. Connect Tailscale on both Waker and User.
  • Check the connected adapter's MAC, firmware and driver. A sent packet does not prove the computer woke.

Report an app error

Include your Selia Wake version, OS, selected role, target connection, exact error and steps to repeat it. Remove QR codes, pairing links and private network details from attachments.

Use GitHub Issues for public bug reports. Send private support and correspondence-removal requests by email.

Accounts and data

Selia Wake has no app accounts, advertising, purchases or subscription. Tailscale accounts are separate. See the data-removal guide and privacy policy.

License and availability

Current original material uses the source-visible proprietary license. Official releases may be installed and used under its terms. Historical MIT grants remain valid. Dependencies keep their licenses.

Download packages from GitHub Releases. Google Play is limited to invited internal testers. Wake capability depends on your hardware, firmware, driver and network. Raspberry Pi architecture and ESP32 gateway requirements are in the device guide.
