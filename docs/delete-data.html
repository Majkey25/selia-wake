Remove your data

Selia Wake has no app account or developer-held server copy of your settings. Manage local data on each device. Tailscale handles its own accounts and data.

Forget a User pairing

In User mode, open Settings, choose Forget pairing, then Save settings. This removes the saved User connection in this installation. App preferences and this installation's own Waker key remain.

Reset Android

  1. Stop The Waker in the app or notification.
  2. Open Android Settings, Apps, Selia Wake, Storage, then Clear data or Clear storage. Labels vary by phone.
  3. Uninstall to remove the app and its private data.

Clearing storage removes settings and keys. Next launch creates a new key. Configure and pair your devices again.

Windows and Linux GUI

Stop The Waker and close the app. Deleting a portable archive or executable can leave OS secure-storage data behind.

Windows stores the current encrypted files under %APPDATA%\MajkeyLab\Selia Wake. Remove that app folder after closing the program to reset local settings. Credential Manager may retain an encryption key. Remove only entries clearly identified as belonging to this app.

Linux uses a Secret Service keyring. Use its keyring manager to remove only entries you can identify as Selia Wake. Do not delete the whole keyring or generic entries shared with other apps. Contact support with your app and OS versions if ownership is unclear.

CLI and ESP32

Stop the CLI relay, then remove its configuration file at the location shown by its help/config commands. The file contains a plaintext secret. Remove your own backup copies too. Uninstalling the executable alone does not delete configuration.

For ESP32, use the firmware's documented USB reset command or erase its flash before transferring the board. Its local nonvolatile storage can retain Wi-Fi credentials and the pairing key. See the device guide.

Copies and correspondence

Forgetting a pairing does not revoke other copies of the same key. The GUI currently has no key-rotation button. Remove your own QR screenshots, saved links and clipboard history, including synced copies.

For access to or deletion of support correspondence, email majkeylab@gmail.com. Do not include pairing keys. The privacy policy describes handling and retention.
