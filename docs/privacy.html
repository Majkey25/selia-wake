Privacy policy

Effective 24 October 2026. Covers Selia Wake for Android, Windows and Linux, and this project website.

Matěj Teplý, publishing as Majkey / MajkeyLab, maintains Selia Wake and handles support correspondence. Privacy questions and requests: majkeylab@gmail.com. Do not send pairing secrets or screenshots of pairing QR codes.

What the app does

Selia Wake sends Wake-on-LAN requests between devices you configure. It has no developer-operated server, app account system, analytics, advertising, billing or tracking SDK. We do not sell app data. The application does not send your settings, pairing keys or wake history to the maintainer.

Information on your devices

The app stores your selected role, appearance, language, device names, network addresses, target MAC address and pairing secrets in operating-system secure storage. These are used to connect the devices you choose and wake the target you configure. Android backup of private app data is disabled. Desktop secure storage can remain after deleting the portable application.

Pairing links and QR codes contain the Waker's address, name and secret key. Copying or scanning them transfers those details to a device you choose. Clipboard reads and writes happen only after the corresponding Paste or Copy action. Your OS clipboard history or configured clipboard sync can retain copies; manage those separately.

Communication between your devices

When you press Wake, the User device sends a timestamp, random nonce and HMAC-SHA256 signature to its paired Waker. The pairing secret itself is not part of that wake request. The Waker sends a local magic packet containing the configured target MAC. These requests go to your chosen devices, not MajkeyLab. A bounded in-memory replay cache rejects nonce reuse for 60 seconds; expired entries are pruned when processing new requests.

Tailscale is a separate application and service, governed by its own privacy policy. It supplies encryption for remote traffic through your tailnet; Selia Wake does not receive your Tailscale account credentials. The relay uses HTTP inside that encrypted connection, so access to the listener should be restricted to trusted peers. The local Wake-on-LAN broadcast is not encrypted.

Camera and other permissions

Android camera access is requested only when you choose Scan QR code. ZXing decodes the camera frames on your device. The app does not save or upload camera images. You can cancel scanning or pair by pasting a link without camera permission.

Network and Wi-Fi permissions allow communication and detection of local network addresses. On supported Android versions, nearby-device permission supports LAN access. Notifications show the visible Waker foreground service and its Stop action; a wake lock supports relay availability while enabled. The app does not request contacts, location, microphone, photo-library or broad file access.

Retention and removal

Local configuration remains until you remove it. Stop The Waker to stop relaying. To remove a User pairing, choose Forget pairing and Save settings. That does not revoke copies of the Waker key on other devices. There is currently no in-app key rotation control.

On Android, clearing app storage or uninstalling removes private app data. Resetting a Waker's app storage generates a new key on its next launch and requires new configuration and pairing. On desktop, deleting the executable alone can leave secure-storage data behind. See data-removal instructions for the available controls and OS-specific cleanup.

There is no Selia Wake account or developer-held server-side app data to delete. Tailscale accounts and their data are managed separately with Tailscale. Copies of pairing codes, screenshots or clipboard history outside the app must be removed separately.

Support correspondence

If you contact support, we receive the address, message and attachments you choose to send. Email providers process that correspondence to deliver and store it. We use it to respond to your request and retain it as needed to resolve the request and meet legal obligations. You can ask for access, correction or removal at the contact above. GitHub issues are public and governed by GitHub's policies; never post private network details or pairing codes there.

Website hosting and external services

This static website adds no cookies, browser storage, analytics, advertising, embedded trackers or third-party fonts. It is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub states that visitors' IP addresses are logged for security purposes; see GitHub Pages data collection and the GitHub privacy statement.

Links to GitHub downloads, Google Play, Tailscale, Microsoft and other external sites open services governed by their own policies. Installation, updates, platform diagnostics or backup features controlled by your operating system or store are separate from Selia Wake's app data handling.

Changes and contact

This page and its effective date will be updated when the app's data handling changes. Contact majkeylab@gmail.com for privacy or support requests. English and Czech support are available.
