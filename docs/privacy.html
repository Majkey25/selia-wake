Selia Wake privacy policy

Effective 24 October 2026. Developer: MajkeyLab.

Selia Wake sends Wake-on-LAN requests between devices you configure. It has no developer-operated server, account, analytics, advertising, or tracking SDK.

Data on your devices

The app stores your settings, device names, network addresses, and target MAC address locally. Pairing secrets are stored using the operating system's secure storage. Android app backup is disabled. You can remove the app's local data through your device settings or by uninstalling it.

Network requests

When you press Wake, the app sends a timestamp, a random nonce, and a cryptographic signature to your paired Waker through Tailscale. Your Waker sends a magic packet containing the target MAC address on your local network. These requests are not sent to MajkeyLab. The Waker keeps a bounded, short-lived nonce cache in memory to prevent repeated requests.

Tailscale is a separate application and service, governed by its own privacy policy. Selia Wake does not receive your Tailscale account credentials.

Permissions

Network and Wi-Fi permissions allow communication with your devices. On Android, the visible Waker foreground service and wake lock keep the relay available while it is enabled. Notifications show the relay state and provide a Stop action. Camera access is requested only when you choose to scan a pairing QR code. QR decoding happens on the device using ZXing; the app does not save or upload camera images. You can pair by pasting a link without camera access. The app does not request access to your contacts, location, microphone, photos, or files.

Control and contact

You can stop the Waker or remove a User pairing in the app. Removing a User pairing does not revoke copies of the Waker's key held by other devices. The current app has no in-app key rotation control; resetting the Waker's Android app data creates a new key and removes its settings. Share pairing links and QR codes only with devices you trust. Questions and privacy requests can be submitted through the project issue tracker. Do not post pairing codes or other private information there.
